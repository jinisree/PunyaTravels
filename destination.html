District-Wise Guide

Kerala Travel Destinations

🌴 South Kerala

South Kerala Thiruvananthapuram District

Thiruvananthapuram

Kovalam Beach, Varkala Cliff, Ponmudi Hill Station, Poovar Backwater Island, Neyyar Dam & Wildlife.

South Kerala Kollam & Pathanamthitta

Kollam & Pathanamthitta

Ashtamudi Lake, Munroe Island, Thenmala Eco-Tourism, Jatayu Earth's Center, Gavi & Konni Elephant center.

🌿 Central Kerala

Central Kerala Alappuzha & Kottayam

Alappuzha & Kottayam

Alleppey Backwaters Houseboats, Marari Beach, Kuttanad Paddy fields, Kumarakom & Vembanad Lake.

Central Kerala Idukki & Ernakulam

Idukki & Ernakulam (Kochi)

Munnar Tea Hills, Thekkady Periyar Wildlife, Vagamon, Ramakkalmedu, Fort Kochi, Mattancherry & Cherai Beach.

🏔️ North & Central Kerala

North & Central Thrissur & Palakkad

Thrissur & Palakkad

Guruvayur Temple, Athirappilly & Vazhachal Waterfalls, Kerala Kalamandalam, Malampuzha Dam, Silent Valley & Parambikulam.

North & Central Malappuram & Kozhikode

Malappuram & Kozhikode

Kottakkunnu, Nilambur Teak museum, Kozhikode Beach, Kappad Beach, Beypore port & Thusharagiri Waterfalls.

🌊 North Kerala

North Kerala Wayanad District

Wayanad Nature Reserve

Banasura Sagar Dam, Edakkal Caves, Soochipara Falls, Pookode Lake, and Chembra Peak trekking.

North Kerala Kannur & Kasaragod

Kannur & Kasaragod

Muzhappilangad Drive-in Beach, St. Angelo Fort, Bekal Fort & Beach, Ranipuram & Valiyaparamba backwaters.

Meenakshi Amman Temple, Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace, Ramanathaswamy Temple, Pamban Bridge & Brihadeeswarar Temple.

Spiritual Circuit Kanchipuram, Trichy & Palani

Kanchipuram, Srirangam & Chidambaram

Kamakshi Amman, Ekambareswarar, Nataraja Temple Chidambaram, Srirangam Ranganathaswamy & Palani Murugan temple.
